In short

  • The developer keeps no data about you: there is no account and no game server.
  • Your game is on your phone: uninstalling the game deletes it.
  • For any request, write to avdev78@gmail.com, mentioning "Builder's Kingdom".

1. Your game and settings

Data: progress, buildings, crowns, gems, sound and tutorial settings, stored only on your phone.

How to delete it: uninstall Builder's Kingdom, or open Android Settings › Apps › Builder's Kingdom › Storage › Clear data. Deletion is immediate and complete; nothing is kept.

2. Google Play Games save (if you turned it on)

Data: your gem balance, saved in your Google Play Games account.

How to delete it: in the Google Play Games app, open your profile › Settings › Delete Play Games account & data, then choose Builder's Kingdom in the list of game data to delete. You can also write to us and we will guide you. The save is deleted as soon as you request it; the developer keeps no copy.

3. Advertising data (Google AdMob)

Data: advertising ID, IP address and device information, processed by Google for ads.

How to delete it: in Android Settings › Google › Ads, choose Delete advertising ID (or "Reset"). Data already processed by Google is covered by its privacy policy; you can also manage it in your Google account.

4. Your purchases

Google Play keeps the history of your gem purchases, as required by law (accounting obligations). The developer keeps no copy. Purchased gems not saved in Play Games are lost when the game data is deleted.

Any question?

Write to avdev78@gmail.com with the subject "Builder's Kingdom – data deletion". We reply within 30 days at most. See also our privacy policy.
