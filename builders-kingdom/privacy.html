Privacy Policy

Updated October 30, 2026 · App io.github.avdev78.builderskingdom

Français · English · Español

In short

  • The game asks for no account, no name and no email address.
  • Your game is saved only on your phone.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which processes some data with your consent.
  • Payments go through Google Play: the game never sees your payment details.

Who is responsible

Builder's Kingdom is a game developed by Alexis, an independent developer. For any question about your data: avdev78@gmail.com

Data stored by the game

Your progress (waves, buildings, upgrades, crowns, gems, sound and tutorial settings) is stored in your phone's memory. It is not sent to the developer. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads, some of which are optional and give a reward. They are provided by Google AdMob, which may process:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address, which gives an approximate location;
  • technical information about the device (model, Android version, language);
  • your interactions with ads (views, clicks).

This data is used to show ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, your choice is requested at first launch through Google's consent form; you can change it at any time from the game menu ("Privacy"). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Google › Ads).

Learn more: how Google uses data from partner apps.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

Gems and ad removal are bought through Google Play's payment system. The game only receives a purchase confirmation to credit you; it never has access to your payment method. These payments are governed by Google's privacy policy.

Google Play Games save (optional)

If you sign in to Google Play Games from the menu, your gem balance is saved in your Google Play Games account so you can get it back after reinstalling or on another phone. No other data is sent.

The game is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data about them.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete and object to the processing of your data. Since the game keeps no data with the developer, these rights are mainly exercised with Google for ads and Google Play. For any question, write to avdev78@gmail.com. To delete your data, follow these steps. You can also file a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

Changes

This policy may change as the game evolves, for example if anonymous gameplay statistics are added. The date at the top of the page shows the latest version.
