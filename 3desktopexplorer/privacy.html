Privacy Policy

Updated November 20, 2026

Français · English

In short

  • 3DesktopExplorer collects no personal data and sends nothing over the internet.
  • It asks for no account, no name and no email address.
  • It has no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting.
  • Everything it reads and stores stays on your computer.

Who is responsible

3DesktopExplorer is developed by Alexis, an independent developer. For any question: avdev78@gmail.com

What the software reads on your computer

To show your desktop in 3D, 3DesktopExplorer reads, on your computer only:

  • the names, icons and thumbnails of the files and folders on your desktop and in the folders you open;
  • the Windows desktop icon settings (to show This PC, the Recycle Bin…);
  • when you search the whole PC, the results of the Windows Search index.

Files are renamed, moved, created or sent to the Recycle Bin only when you ask for it.

What the software stores

The layout of your icons, your groups (cubes and spheres), your saved views and tabs, your window positions and an icon cache are stored in the software's settings folder on your computer. They are never sent to the developer. Uninstalling the software removes them.

Network

3DesktopExplorer works entirely offline. Updates of the Microsoft Store version are handled by Microsoft Store, under Microsoft's privacy statement.

Children

The software does not knowingly collect any data, including about children.

Your rights

Since the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to access or delete on the developer's side. For any question, write to avdev78@gmail.com. You can also contact your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

Changes

This policy may change if the software evolves, for example if an online feature is added. The date at the top of the page shows the latest version.
